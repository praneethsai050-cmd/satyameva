# SIH Presentation Format
When asked to generate a Smart India Hackathon (SIH) PPT or presentation, strictly adhere to this 6-slide structure extracted from the official SIH 2025 template:

- **Slide 1: Title & Flow** 
  - Hackathon Name, PS ID, PS Title, Theme, Category, Team Details (ID, Name, Members).
  - User Flow Chart (visual diagram of primary actors).
- **Slide 2: Proposed Solution** 
  - Innovation and Uniqueness (4-5 bold points explaining novel features).
  - Technical Flowcharts (Architecture/Data flow diagrams).
- **Slide 3: Technical Approach**
  - System Design Explanation (3-4 bullet points).
  - Tech Stack broken down by: Frontend, Backend, Database/Deployment, Security.
- **Slide 4: Feasibility and Viability**
  - Feasibility Analysis (Technical & Economic).
  - Challenges and Solutions (Table or columns mapping specific challenges to structural solutions).
- **Slide 5: Impact and Benefits**
  - Potential Impact on Target Audience.
  - Benefits of the Solution (Scalability, efficiency, etc.).
- **Slide 6: Research and References**
  - 4-5 core technical or academic references validating the methodology (e.g., specific algorithms, security protocols).
