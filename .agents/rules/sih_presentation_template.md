# Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Presentation Deck Standard

When generating presentations, idea templates, or pitch decks for Smart India Hackathon (SIH):

1. **Aspect Ratio & Layout**:
   - Always use 16:9 widescreen landscape layout (1440x810 pt or 13.333" x 7.5").
   - Never generate single-column vertical A4 portrait documents for SIH idea templates.

2. **Standard 6-Slide Structure**:
   - **Slide 1: Cover & Workflow** — Problem Statement ID, Title, Theme, Category, Team ID, Team Name, Member Roster, and 3-Branch Process Workflow (Issuer -> Student -> Public Verifier).
   - **Slide 2: Problem & USP** — Problem statement tagline, "What is [Project]?", Blockchain Lifecycle flowchart, and 6-pill USP cluster (Decentralized, Instant, Low Cost, Cryptographic Tamper-Proof, Self-Sovereign, Revocable).
   - **Slide 3: Technical Approach** — 5-stage tech stack pipeline (Frontend, Backend, Database, Security, Blockchain/AI), 3-step Methodology, 5-step Implementation Flow ladder, and Mobile/Web UI mockups.
   - **Slide 4: Feasibility & Viability** — 3 KPI metric cards (~2s mint, ₹0.08 cost, total budget), 4-Phase Rollout Roadmap (Pilot -> State -> Mandate -> National), and Risk & Mitigation Matrix.
   - **Slide 5: Impact & Benefits** — 4 high-impact quantitative stats, 3 Key Stakeholder impacts (Students, Institutions, Employers), and 4 Societal Benefits (Social, Economic, Environmental, Policy).
   - **Slide 6: Research & References** — 5 formal academic/standards citations (e.g. UGC, NIST FIPS 180-4, RFC 2104, Blockcerts, Framework docs) with strictly aligned purpose mappings.

3. **Deliverables**:
   - Provide both editable PowerPoint (`.pptx`) and ready-to-upload PDF (`.pdf`).
