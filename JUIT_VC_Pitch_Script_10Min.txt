# 🛡️ Phase 4: Anticipated VC & Faculty Q&A Playbook (8:00 – 10:00)
*(Bulletproof Answers for the Vice Chancellor and Senior Faculty)*

---

#### ❓ Q1: "Why did you build a custom blockchain instead of using existing public networks like Ethereum, Solana, or Polygon?"
> **Answer (Praneeth Sai)**:
> *"Sir, public blockchains like Ethereum or Solana suffer from volatile transaction gas fees and external network congestion. Forcing a university to pay fluctuating crypto gas fees for issuing degrees is practically unfeasible for academic administration.*
>
> *We engineered an in-house Proof-of-Work blockchain (SHA-256 difficulty 4) that runs within the university consortium with **zero gas fees** and deterministic transaction speeds. For external public auditing, we have designed a Polygon L2 state-anchor bridge that costs only ₹0.08 per 10,000 certificates, combining consortium privacy with public verification."*

---

#### ❓ Q2: "How does this compare with the Government's DigiLocker and National Academic Depository (NAD)?"
> **Answer (Shashank Ray)**:
> *"Sir, DigiLocker and NAD are centralized storage depositories — they store PDFs provided by universities. However, DigiLocker itself does not cryptographically prevent an insider with database access from replacing a PDF or tampering with a record before ingestion.*
>
> *Satyameva Aegis is a **cryptographic trust protocol**, not just a storage drive. We provide the cryptographic proof-of-issuance at the point of origin. In fact, our architecture is designed to integrate seamlessly via REST API bridges into DigiLocker and NAD, acting as their cryptographic validation backbone."*

---

#### ❓ Q3: "What is the computational and infrastructural cost for JUIT to run this?"
> **Answer (Praneeth Sai)**:
> *"Sir, the infrastructure footprint is exceptionally lightweight. Because of our optimized SQLite-WAL and multi-tier cryptographic design, the entire Satyameva node runs smoothly on standard university server hardware with as little as 2 GB of RAM.*
>
> *During our testing, mining a new credential block took under 1.8 seconds on a standard dual-core CPU with zero CPU throttling. For JUIT, hosting this system requires zero new capital expenditure in hardware."*

---

#### ❓ Q4: "How reliable is Google Gemini AI for parsing varied marksheet formats?"
> **Answer (Praneeth Sai)**:
> *"Sir, Gemini 2.0 Flash is a state-of-the-art multimodal model. It doesn't rely on rigid OCR coordinate templates; it understands document context semantically. Whether a certificate has a vertical, horizontal, or multi-column layout, Gemini accurately extracts the roll number, student name, and CGPA.*
>
> *Crucially, we maintain a **human-in-the-loop** design: the extracted fields are pre-filled for the Registrar to review and confirm before the cryptographic block is mined onto the blockchain, guaranteeing 100% data fidelity."*

---

#### ❓ Q5: "What if a student loses their login or someone tries to screenshot the QR code?"
> **Answer (Praneeth Sai)**:
> *"Sir, our QR codes are cryptographically linked directly to the permanent blockchain record (`src=qr`). Even if a screenshot is shared, scanning it will only ever show the immutable academic record as verified by JUIT — it cannot be edited, transferred, or counterfeited.*
>
> *Furthermore, for sensitive corporate checks, our system also supports 60-second dynamic HMAC ephemeral tokens that expire after one minute to prevent replay attacks."*
