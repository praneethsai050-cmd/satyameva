In India, over 40,000 fake degrees and forged academic documents are detected every year. Manual background checks take 2 to 4 weeks and cost thousands of rupees per applicant. 

Satyameva Aegis solves this national crisis permanently by transforming physical university certificates into self-sovereign, tamper-proof blockchain assets. Employers and background-check agencies can verify credentials in under 2 seconds using a cryptographically anchored, permanent institutional QR code.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✨ KEY FEATURES DEMONSTRATED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. Google Gemini 2.0 Flash AI Extraction:
   Registrars simply upload a marksheet/certificate image or PDF. Gemini AI automatically extracts student name, roll number, course, and exam details, reducing issuance time from minutes to seconds.

2. Proof-of-Work Blockchain Ledger:
   Every credential is cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 100,000 iterations and a per-document random salt, then mined into an immutable ledger dual-persisted for 100% data integrity.

3. Permanent Institutional QR Code:
   Features official Jaypee campus crests (JIIT, JUIT, JUET) with Error Correction Level H (~30%) and a revolving cyber radar HUD animation. QR codes are lifetime-valid and never expire.

4. Self-Sovereign Student Portal:
   Students own a digital wallet of all issued credentials, with 3D interactive card-flip ledger inspectors and one-click Stamped PDF certificate downloads.

5. Instant Public Verification (Under 2 Seconds):
   Employers verify credentials on `/verify` without needing accounts. The system re-derives the cryptographic hash from scratch, ensuring zero silent passes for altered database records (immediate TAMPERED verdict).

6. Automated Gmail SMTP Notifications:
   Real-time email alerts sent to students on credential issuance and verification checks from hackathonsage@gmail.com.

7. Unified Super Admin Control Center:
   Multi-campus onboarding, role-based access control, document audit logs, and live blockchain block explorer.
