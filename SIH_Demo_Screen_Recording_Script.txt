## 🎯 Pro Tips for a Winning Video

1. **Pacing**: Don't rush. Speak at a measured, calm tempo. 5 minutes and 20 seconds is the sweet spot.
2. **Cursor Movement**: Move your mouse smoothly. Hover over buttons for 1 second before clicking so the jury's eyes can follow.
3. **No Terminal Windows Needed**: Since Satyameva runs seamlessly in desktop app mode, you don't need to show any ugly black command prompt windows during your recording.
4. **Volume**: Do a 15-second mic sound-check first to ensure your voice is crisp and audible over any background noise.
